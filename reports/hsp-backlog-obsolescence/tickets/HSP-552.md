# HSP-552: Same Day Booking Truncated vs Time Based Dates

## Claim

The premise: guest-api sends day-only dates to price-api, so rate plans with a minimum lead time in hours get evaluated against midnight instead of the
real check-in time, and same-day bookings are wrongly (in)applicable. If solved, a same-day quote would use a check-in time of now plus one minute once
the default check-in time has passed, and lead time would be computed from that time.

## Evidence

- RC-1338 ("Same Day Booking On Website Issue") has a description identical to this ticket's, resolution Done, resolved 2024-06-28. This ticket is a copy left open.
- `price-api` @ `a6e967f2`, `service/src/utils/date-time-utils.ts:196-240`: `createCheckInAndCheckOutDateTime` builds the check-in time from the property's default
  check-in time. For a same-day booking after the default check-in time and before the advance notice cut-off, it uses `DateTime.now().plus({ minutes: 1 })` (line 233).
  Introduced `64fecbb9`, 2023-06-27, RC-858 (#940), before this ticket was written.
- `service/src/quotes/service.ts:1559-1591` (`mapRoomTypesToDefaultCheckInCheckOutTimes`) applies it, and `service/src/price-options/service.ts:160` and `:350` call it for
  both the bulk and the single price-options paths, which is what guest-api's availability search calls (`guest-api` @ `1674a1f1`
  `service/src/components/availability/pricing-modules/price-api/price-options.pricing-module.ts`).
- `revenue-management` @ `15e78246`, `service/src/rate-plan-applications/rate-plan-applications.helpers.ts:152-155`: `leadTimeHours` is the difference between the time-aware
  check-in datetime and the quote time, and `rate-plan-application.applicability-filter.ts:441-442` compares it to `minimumLeadTimeHours`. So lead time is not computed from a truncated date.
- Design differs from the ticket: the time logic lives in price-api, not in guest-api as the ticket wanted. The ticket's problem (wrong lead time on same-day quotes) no longer happens, which is what counts.
- Not checked: the exact commit that closed RC-1338 (no commit message mentions RC-1338 in price-api or guest-api), and behavior in the edge case between cut-off and midnight.
- Repos outside `default-branches.tsv` (`price-api`, `revenue-management`) were read from `origin/master` (price-api local clone) and `gh api` (revenue-management).
