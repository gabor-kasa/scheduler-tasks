## Claim

If this ticket were done, the checkout page would show a Google sign-in option when the typed email belongs to a guest account,
and signing in would carry the checkout session over to the new auth session (guestId, inquiry, billingAccountId, other tabs).
What would make it moot: another way for such a guest to sign in at checkout that solves "I can't log in with email and password".
