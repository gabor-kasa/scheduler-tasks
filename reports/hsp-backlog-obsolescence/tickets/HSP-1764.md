# HSP-1764: Provide an option to the guest to set the selected card in failed payment flow as default incidental card

- **Verdict:** partial, product question
- **Label:** backlog-review-unclear
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Checked:** 2026-10-19, scheduled-task batch
- **Jira before write:** Story, created 2024-11-07, updated 2025-02-13, no labels, no Repository field, status To Do, parent HSP-1672 "Damage hold to add-on service", no links, no comments. The acceptance criteria were never filled in ("Requirements...").

## Claim

Done means: when a guest retries a failed add-on payment (damage hold or other add-on) with another card, the guest can choose to make that card the reservation's default incidental card. What would make it moot: the paid card becomes the incidental card without a choice.

## Evidence

- Automatic, not optional. `add-on-service` @ 6e01fea `service/src/stripe/stripe.service.ts:277` puts `saveCardFor: CardUseFor.INCIDENTAL` in the metadata of every add-on payment intent. Commit 36a04a5 (2026-03-20, #617, "always send saveCardFor as INCIDENTAL") added it.
- The consumer saves it. `stripe-sync` @ 2851860 `service/src/components/stripe/payment-intent/payment-intent.service.ts:113-117` reads `metadata.saveCardFor` on `payment_intent.succeeded`, and `saveCardFromPaymentMethod` (line 339) inserts the card with `defaultFor: [saveCardFor]` and attaches it to the reservation. So the card that finally pays an add-on becomes the incidental card.
- No choice in the UI. `guest-portal` @ 7cce37fb `components/add-ons/payment-page/AddOnPaymentPagePayment.vue:15-23` renders `PaymentFormWithWallet` with no "use as default" control, and a search for default-card controls in guest-portal components and pages finds none.
- Searched: add-on-service, stripe-sync, guest-portal, guest-api. Not checked: what happens when the retry uses an already stored card (whether `insertCard` duplicates it), and the failed-payment screens in kasa-app.

## Note for review

Adam: the outcome the ticket wants (the card that worked becomes the incidental card) now happens for every add-on payment, but silently, and the ticket asked for an option. Question for you: is the automatic overwrite acceptable, or do guests need an opt-in? If it is acceptable, close this. If a guest's first card was meant to stay the incidental card, the 2026-03 change may be a regression worth its own ticket.
