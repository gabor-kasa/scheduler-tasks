## Claim

Done means: a "Change booking" link in the VFD "Booking details" section (hidden for cancelled or ended reservations), a subpage with a "Request a change" popup (change dates, change room, other, plus free text), a new `POST /booking/:confirmationCode/modify` in guest-api that sends a `guest_modify_reservation` payload to Kustomer the way `check-out` does, and the matching guest permission. What would make it moot: the same request handled through another guest-api endpoint or the support section.

## Evidence

- No modify endpoint. `guest-api` @ c0d40e92 `service/src/components/booking/booking.routes.ts` lists every booking route. The set is `/`, `/review-tags`, `/:code`, `child-reservations`, `accesses`, `payment-plan`, `property-information`, `cii`, `coi`, `review`, `kbc`, `extension`, `caa`, `invoices`, `check-out` (line 195), `unit`, `do-not-disturb`, `cancellation-eligibility`, `cancel` (line 233), `cancellation-policy`, `support-section`, `wallet`, `service-requests`, `guest`. There is no `modify`.
- No Kustomer call for it. `service/src/libs/kustomer/` exports `requestCancellation`, `checkOut`, `requestExtensionDate`, `declineInquiry`, `sendReceiptRequest` and `sendServiceRequest`. Nothing for modification. `git log -S guest_modify_reservation` on guest-api returns no commit.
- No frontend. `guest-portal` @ 7cce37fb has `ManageExtensionWidget.vue` (extension, via `canModifyBookingLength.extension`) but no change-booking link. A search for `modify booking`, `change booking`, `change my room`, `change my dates`, `request a change` in guest-portal, guest-api and kasa-app found nothing relevant (the `change-dates` hits are search-bar CTAs on the booking flow, not the guest's existing booking).
- Searched: guest-api, guest-portal, kasa-app, kasa-website. Did not search repos outside the HSP set.
- The related VFD work did ship in the same area: extension requests, cancel, service requests. The "modify" request is the one left behind.
