## Claim

If this were done, the reservation kobject kustomer-sync writes into Kustomer would carry two custom fields, one for the company id and one for the company name, so agents and Kustomer rules can tell which white-label company a reservation belongs to.

## Evidence

- `kustomer-sync` @ `f0b54f0c`, `utils/convertKustomerReservation.ts:53`: `companyIdStr: reservation.companyId?.toString()` is on every reservation kobject. Typed at `libs/kustomer/kustomer-types.d.ts:239`. Projected from Mongo in `libs/mongoWrapper.ts:82,116`, so the field exists end to end.
- Dated with `git log -S companyIdStr`: introduced by `8d3d3fd` (2024-04-08, "HSP-912 - Send companyId of reservation", #549). HSP-912 "Send the companyId to KUstomer when rez is created" is Done, resolved 2024-04-08. It is a separate ticket, so HSP-746 is partly a duplicate of a closed one.
- No company name on the reservation kobject. Searching `kustomer-sync` and `kustomer-cards` for `company` finds `companyName?: string` only at `libs/kustomer/kustomer-types.d.ts:128`, which is in the customer type, not `ReservationAttributes.custom`. `convertKustomerReservation.ts` has no `companyName` anywhere. The service is live (last commit `f0b54f0`, 2026-10-12; repo not archived).
- Not checked: whether Kustomer has a company-id to name lookup configured on the Kustomer side (outside the repo), which would make the name unnecessary.
