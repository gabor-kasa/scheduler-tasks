## Claim

If this ticket were done, reservation-risk-service would handle the `guestMerged` event and, when the merged guest is verified
but some of the guest's reservations are not, set the reservations' guest-verified field to true.

## Evidence

  - `libs/guestVerificationService.ts:23-54` loads the guest's upcoming and ongoing reservations and calls `updateGuestIsVerified(reservation, true, reason)` on each.
  - `libs/reservationClient.ts:145-173` writes `infinityStones.guestIsVerified` with an audit entry. The ticket spells the
    field `isGuestVerified`. The real field is `guestIsVerified`, so that is a typo in the ticket, not a missing piece.
  - Unit test: `functions/onGuestMerged/handler.unit.test.ts`.
