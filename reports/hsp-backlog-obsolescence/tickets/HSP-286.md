assignee: yellow for pending, green for accepted, read from the task's assignment status.

    "Assignees" as plain text. The only chip in the task section is the task status (line 147),
    not the per-assignee acceptance.
  - No commit on the calendar pages mentions acceptance or assignment status (`git log -G`).
- The data is already available:
  - `breezeway-sync` @ origin/master `4c75e73`, `functions/handle-task-change.ts:178` stores
    `status: assignment.type_task_user_status` per assignee.
  - `breezeway-sync-api-client` @ origin/master, `src/models/Task.ts:18-22` exposes
    `assignees[].status?: 'assigned' | 'accepted'`.
